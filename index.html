Controls
In VR — reach to the tray on your right and squeeze to pick up a tool; squeeze again to put it back. Trigger sculpts with whatever you're holding. Bare hands smooth with a fingertip and pull clay when you squeeze. Grip away from the tray holds and turns the ball; both grips scale it. Thumbstick resizes the brush · A undoes.
Hand tracking — pinch to sculpt, same tray.
Desktop — click a tool on the tray to pick it up, or the bare tray to put it down · drag on the clay to sculpt · drag off it (or right-drag) to orbit · scroll to zoom · 1–8 pick tools · [ ] resize · X mirrors · space spins · Ctrl+Z undoes.
